Your Aggregate

Fill in at least 6 passing grades (A1–E8) to see your aggregate.

Your aggregate is the sum of your best 6 subjects (lowest point total). A1 = 1 point, F9 is a fail and isn't counted.

This is a general estimate, not an official result. Actual cut-off points vary by school, programme, and year — always confirm with CSSPS guidelines and your target school.
